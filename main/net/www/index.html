LoTW setup — step 1 of 2: your certificate file

The Tab5 signs and uploads QSOs itself — it just needs a one-time copy of your LoTW callsign certificate as a .p12 file. You make that file with TQSL on your PC (takes a minute):

  1. Start TQSL and open the Callsign Certificates tab.
  2. Click your callsign, then Save the Callsign Certificate.
  3. Pick a location, set a password when asked, and save the .p12 file.

Next opens ARRL's own illustrated instructions in a new tab and continues to the import step. No LoTW account yet? Start at ARRL's Getting Started guide first, then come back here.
