# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in the QMX Panadapter.

## Code contributions are not being accepted right now

This is a **solo project**. All coding is currently done by the author
(Steffen Lav, OZ1LAV), at his own pace, to keep the design coherent.

**Please do not open pull requests** — they will be politely declined. This is a
deliberate choice while the project is still finding its shape, not a judgement
on anyone's work. The policy may change as the project matures; if it does, this
file will be updated.

## Feedback is very welcome

Using the panadapter and telling me how it goes is the most useful thing you can
do right now:

- **Bug reports** — something broke, behaved oddly, or crashed
- **Feature requests & ideas** — what would make it better for your operating
- **Field reports** — what worked (and what didn't) on your hardware/band/setup

Please send these to either:

- **GitHub Issues:** https://github.com/SteffenLav/qmx-panadapter/issues
- **QRPLabs Groups.io thread:** https://groups.io/g/QRPLabs/topic/119565643

A good bug report includes your Tab5 model, QMX/QMX+ firmware version, app
version, steps to reproduce, and a diagnostic log
(Settings → Diagnostic log → on, reproduce, then download).

## Building and modifying for yourself

This project is [MIT-licensed](LICENSE) — you are free to build, flash, fork, and
modify your own copy however you like. The policy above only concerns merging
changes back into *this* repository; what you do with your own fork is up to you.
