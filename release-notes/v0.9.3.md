## Highlights  Diagnostic-only release. Adds a marker-fenced **TAB5 BSP INFO** block to the boot log so users with display / touch issues can quickly identify which Tab5 hardware revision they have.  ## Why  The M5Stack Tab5 ships in (at least) two hardware variants that have no visible marker on the back of the unit:  - **ST7123 panel + ST7123 touch** - Tab5 v1.3 ECO2 (what this firmware currently supports) - **ILI9881C panel + GT911 touch** - older variant (not yet supported; tracked for a future release)  If v0.9.2 didn''t work on your Tab5 (frequency stuck at default, web UI disconnected, only the burger menu reactive), please flash v0.9.3 and share the **TAB5 BSP INFO** block from your boot log. That data lets us scope the work needed to support the second hardware variant.  ## How to capture the boot log  1. Flash this release via [web.esphome.io](https://web.esphome.io/) using the merged `.bin` attached below 2. After flashing, the device boots. Connect over USB serial (115200 baud) and look for the block near the top of the log:          I (xxxx) bsp_info: === TAB5 BSP INFO ===         I (xxxx) bsp_info: chip:     ESP32-P4 rev vX.Y         I (xxxx) bsp_info: psram:    NN MB         I (xxxx) bsp_info: panel:    ... (inferred from touch)         I (xxxx) bsp_info: touch:    ... @ 0xNN         I (xxxx) bsp_info: heap:     ...         I (xxxx) bsp_info: idf:      v5.4.4         I (xxxx) bsp_info: firmware: v0.9.3         I (xxxx) bsp_info: =====================  3. Open an issue at https://github.com/SteffenLav/qmx-panadapter/issues with the block pasted in.  ## What's in this release  - New `bsp_info_log()` called once at boot after display init - Probes touch IC by I2C (ST7123 at 0x55, GT911 at 0x5D / 0x14) and infers the paired panel - Read-only and failure-tolerant - never touches the MIPI-DSI bus, never panics on a NACK - No behavioural change to the panadapter itself; v0.9.2 functionality is bundled unchanged  ## Known issues  - Settings stored in the default `nvs` partition may be wiped by merged-binary reflashes (fixed in v0.9.4)  ## Flashing  Use the merged `qmx_panadapter_v0.9.3.bin` (attached) at [web.esphome.io](https://web.esphome.io/).  ---  **Full changelog:** [v0.9.2...v0.9.3](https://github.com/SteffenLav/qmx-panadapter/compare/v0.9.2...v0.9.3)  73 de OZ1LAV
