# v0.11.0 - Pinch-Zoom, Pan, and CW Frequency Improvements

## What's new

### Pinch-zoom and two-finger pan
The spectrum and waterfall now support pinch-to-zoom (two-finger spread/pinch) and two-finger drag-to-pan. Zoom range is x1.0 (full 48 kHz view) to x24.0. Pan resets to centre on every tune. Double-tap anywhere on the spectrum or waterfall resets zoom and pan to x1.0/0.

- Zoom indicator in the top bar: dim grey at x1.0, amber when zoomed in ("Zoom: x12.1")
- Zoom level persisted to NVS across power cycles
- Frequency axis labels update in real time as you zoom and pan, with Hz resolution at high zoom (span < 10 kHz)
- Passband indicator lines follow zoom and pan correctly
- Tap-to-tune works correctly at any zoom level; snap radius scales with zoom

### Floating frequency tooltip
While dragging to tune, a cyan label above the finger shows the target frequency in real time (MM.KKK.HHH format). In CW mode the shown frequency snaps to the nearest 10 Hz, matching the tune resolution.

### Cyan cursor extended to waterfall
The cyan tune-cursor line is now visible across the waterfall area as well as the spectrum.

### CW offset read from QMX via CAT
The CW LO offset (default 700 Hz) is now read directly from the QMX at connect time via `MMCW|CW offset;` rather than being hardcoded. A per-unit trim slider ("CW trim", ±100 Hz, 5 Hz steps, default -60 Hz) allows calibration to match individual QMX units. Both the offset and the trim are applied only in CW mode; all other modes are unaffected.

### CW touch-to-tune behaviour corrected
The CW sidetone pitch correction has been removed from the touch-to-tune math. Tapping a signal in the waterfall now tunes the dial to that signal's frequency in all modes, consistent with SDR convention. The CW offset is handled entirely by the QMX's own LO shift.
