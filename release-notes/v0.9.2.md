## Highlights  The spectrum trace now optionally renders against a per-bin tracked noise floor, with the noise carpet collapsed to a calm baseline and real signals popping sharp above. Matches the design mockup and lines up visually with the waterfall colours.  ## What's new  ### Flat-spectrum mode Identical algorithm browser-side and device-side: - Temporal EMA on incoming bins - Asymmetric per-bin EMA floor - slow-up so signals don''t drag their own floor; faster-down to track noise - 5-bin spatial smoothing on the rendered trace - Floor bias to lift the visible zero above the canvas bottom  Toggleable both sides: browser via `f` keypress, device via a **Flat Spectrum** switch in the settings drawer. NVS-persisted on the device.  ### Screenshot mutex fix (originally v0.9.1) The Phase 5.11 screenshot tool used a non-blocking display lock and could capture a partially-rendered frame - visible as a wrapped bottom status bar in earlier hero images. Switched to `bsp_display_lock(portMAX_DELAY)` - the snapshot now waits for in-flight LVGL operations to finish.  ## Known issues  - The -30 dBm / -130 dBm labels at the left edge of the spectrum still render in flat mode. They are misleading there because the axis is dB-above-floor, not absolute dBm. Hidden in a follow-up patch. - Settings stored in the default `nvs` partition may be wiped by merged-binary reflashes (fixed in v0.9.4)  ## Flashing  Merged binary `qmx_panadapter-v0.9.2-merged.bin` attached below.
