## Highlights  UI polish release. No new radio functionality, but the device looks and feels a lot more like a finished product.  ## What's new  ### Bottom status bar Replaces the dev-only `FPS / PSRAM / IRAM` line with two things the operator actually cares about:  - **Battery** - level percentage with a charging indicator - **WiFi** - connected SSID and RSSI in dBm; shows `WiFi off` when not connected  Battery readout is currently stubbed (shows a fixed value). Real INA226 wiring follows in the next release; the UI is already in its final shape.  ### Settings drawer rework - Width bumped from 400 px to 520 px so labels and buttons breathe - **IQ Balance** moved up under the title (was crammed near the close X) - **Presets** (HF Normal / HF DX / Strong Sig) laid out side-by-side in a single row instead of stacked - **WiFi** button stays full-width on its own row - **On-screen keyboard** buttons darker for better contrast against the modal backdrop  ### Larger fonts Top-bar and bottom-bar text bumped from Montserrat 20 to Montserrat 24 to match the rest of the UI.  ## Known issues  - Battery indicator shows a fixed value (87%) until INA226 driver lands - Stale `ui_set_db_labels_internal` declaration warning at build time (cosmetic, two-line fix pending)  ## Flashing  Merged binary `qmx_panadapter-v0.8.1-merged.bin` attached below. Drop into [web.esphome.io](https://web.esphome.io/) from Chrome or Edge.  WiFi credentials from a prior install are preserved in NVS.  ## Acknowledgements  Battery indicator architecture inspired by [qrp_companion](https://groups.io/g/QRPLabs/topic/118645485) by Zhenxing Han (N6HAN), who documented the INA226 reading approach and polarity quirk on the Tab5.  ---  **Full changelog:** [v0.8.0...v0.8.1](https://github.com/SteffenLav/qmx-panadapter/compare/v0.8.0...v0.8.1)  73 de OZ1LAV
